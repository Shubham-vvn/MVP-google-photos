How AI Memory Works

Photos show what happened. AI helps you find what it meant to you.

⚠️

The Problem Today

Google Photos is great at finding objects like "beach", "cake", or "dog". However, humans don't remember their lives in keywords. We remember stories:

  • "Mom's surprise 60th birthday with the grandkids in Bangalore"
  • "That weekend Rocky the dog saw snow for the first time"
  • "Reunion catch-up with Priya and Arjun in Goa after 2 years"

Standard search fails because your camera only took pictures — the story stayed in your head.

✨

1

2

3

Search Naturally

Months or years later, search using whatever you recall. Your photos appear right away.
